## Key Themes

*To be populated as sources are ingested.* Likely themes for a Carnatic music wiki:

1. **Lakshya vs Lakshana** — the tension between performance practice and codified theory
2. **The compositional canon** — the Trinity (Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri) and the layers of pre-Trinity, post-Trinity, and modern repertoire
3. **Bani / sampradaya** — how the same raga, kriti, or technique varies across schools
4. **Manodharma** — the improvisational pillars (alapana, niraval, kalpanaswara, ragam-tanam-pallavi) and how they interact with composed material
5. **The instrument question** — vocal primacy and how each accompanying / solo instrument adapts vocal idiom
6. **Transmission** — guru-shishya parampara, notation conventions, recording era

These are placeholders. The actual themes will emerge from the sources you choose to ingest.
